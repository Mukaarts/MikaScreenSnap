First release in the Mac App Store.

• Full screen, region and window capture, each on its own global shortcut
• An annotation editor that opens straight onto the capture — eleven tools, one letter each
• Blur and pixelate replace the saved original, not just the exported copy
• Text extraction in English, German and French, recognised on your own Mac
• An 8× colour loupe and a full-screen pixel ruler
• Pinned screenshots that float above everything and survive a restart
• Auto-save into a folder you choose, with a searchable history
• No Dock icon, no account, no network access
