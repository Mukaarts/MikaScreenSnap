Mika+ScreenSnap takes the screenshot and hands you the pen. Press Control-Shift-Command-4, drag a region, and the annotation editor is already open on it — before you have switched windows.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

Two things set this edition apart from the direct download, and both are worth knowing in advance. It asks you once, during setup, for a folder to save into: a sandboxed app has no path to your Pictures folder it can take on its own, and filing your screenshots inside a hidden container folder would leave them somewhere you would never look. And if you are moving over from the direct download, nothing comes with you — not your settings, not your pinned images. Your files stay exactly where they are, untouched; this edition simply cannot see them.

THREE WAYS TO CAPTURE
Full screen, a dragged region, or a single window — each on its own global shortcut, straight from the menu bar. Point at the window you want, or take the frontmost one outright.

ELEVEN ANNOTATION TOOLS
Arrow, rectangle, ellipse, line, freehand, text, highlight, blur, pixelate, selection and a ruler. Hold Shift to snap to 45°, squares and circles. Every tool is one letter away.

REDACTION THAT ACTUALLY REDACTS
Blur a password and it is gone from the saved file too, not only from the copy you export — even if you close the editor without exporting. The readable original never stays behind on disk.

TEXT EXTRACTION
Drag over any region and the recognised text lands on your clipboard. English, German and French, recognised on your own Mac.

COLOUR PICKER
A magnifying loupe follows the cursor at 8× zoom. Click to copy, Shift-click to keep it in a palette, with a history of the last ten.

PIXEL MEASUREMENT
A full-screen ruler with guide lines, in point-to-point or rectangle mode. Available inside the editor as well — and never part of the exported image.

PINNED SCREENSHOTS
Float any capture above every other window. Scroll to fade it, Shift-drag to resize. Pins survive a restart, and closing one deletes it rather than hiding it.

AUTO-SAVE AND HISTORY
Captures are filed away by themselves. A searchable thumbnail browser is one shortcut away.

YOURS TO CONFIGURE
Rebind every shortcut with conflict detection, set the annotation defaults, choose the output folder and format, and start at login.

MENU BAR ONLY
No Dock icon, no window of its own, no background service. It sits in the menu bar and stays out of the way.


NOTHING LEAVES YOUR MAC

No account, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads. This edition makes no network connection at all — it does not even carry an updater, because the App Store handles that. Your screenshots are files in a folder you chose, and nowhere else.

Screen recording is the only permission it asks for, and it is what a screenshot is: reading what is on your screen. macOS grants it in System Settings, and setup walks you through it.
